## Background

I am currently a Postdoctoral Fellow at [Rice University, Department of Computational Applied Mathematics and Operations Research](https://cmor.rice.edu/), working under the supervision of [Dr. Andrew Schaefer](https://profiles.rice.edu/faculty/andrew-schaefer). Previously, I was a Postdoctoral Fellow at the [University of Toronto, Department of Mechanical and Industrial Engineering](https://mie.utoronto.ca/), where I worked under the supervision of [Dr. Vahid Sarhangian](https://sarhangian.mie.utoronto.ca/), [Dr. Andre Cire](https://utsc.utoronto.ca/mgmt/andre-cire), and [Dr. Adam Diamant](https://schulich.yorku.ca/faculty/adam-diamant/). I received my Ph.D. in Industrial Engineering from [Texas A&M University](https://engineering.tamu.edu/industrial/index.html) in 2024, where I specialized in operations research and public health disease mitigation strategies under the supervision of [Dr. Hrayer Aprahamian](https://engineering.tamu.edu/industrial/profiles/aprahamian-hrayer.html).

---
## Research Interests

My research spans **combinatorial optimization**, **network and graph analysis**, **Markov decision processes**, and **approximate dynamic programming**, with applications in **healthcare decision-making**. I develop optimization-based frameworks that integrate individual-level data, dynamic behavioral changes, and fairness considerations to design efficient and equitable disease mitigation strategies.
