## Background

I hold a joint appointment as a **Postdoctoral Fellow** in the [Department of Computational Applied Mathematics and Operations Research at Rice University](https://cmor.rice.edu/) and a **Research Collaborator** at [The University of Texas MD Anderson Cancer Center](https://www.mdanderson.org/). I work with [Dr. Andrew J. Schaefer](https://profiles.rice.edu/faculty/andrew-schaefer) at Rice and Dr. Iakovos Toumazis at MD Anderson.

Previously, I was a Postdoctoral Fellow in the [Department of Mechanical and Industrial Engineering at the University of Toronto](https://mie.utoronto.ca/), working with [Dr. Vahid Sarhangian](https://sarhangian.mie.utoronto.ca/), [Dr. André Augusto Ciré](https://utsc.utoronto.ca/mgmt/andre-cire), and [Dr. Adam Diamant](https://schulich.yorku.ca/faculty/adam-diamant/). I earned my Ph.D. in Industrial Engineering from [Texas A&M University](https://engineering.tamu.edu/industrial/index.html) in 2024 under the supervision of [Dr. Hrayer Aprahamian](https://engineering.tamu.edu/industrial/profiles/aprahamian-hrayer.html).

## Research areas

<div class="profile-grid">
  <div class="profile-card">
    <h3>Optimization under uncertainty</h3>
    <p>Robust and stochastic methods for decisions that must perform well when data, demand, or system behavior is uncertain.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="profile-card">
    <h3>Sequential decisions</h3>
    <p>Markov decision processes, approximate dynamic programming, and decomposition methods for large decision spaces.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="profile-card">
    <h3>Healthcare operations</h3>
    <p>Decision support for cancer screening and diagnosis, imaging operations, patient sequencing, and public-health policy.</p>
  </div>
</div>

My methodological interests also include combinatorial and network optimization. Across these areas, I focus on models that connect rigorous optimization with consequential, implementable decisions.

<div class="profile-note"><strong>Proposal experience:</strong> I have collaborated on the development of two National Institutes of Health proposals and one National Science Foundation proposal.</div>

## Current research

- **Cancer imaging operations:** Digital-twin and optimization-guided MRI patient sequencing to reduce changeovers and increase appointment-slot availability; submitted to *JCO Oncology Practice* and selected for Best of Professional at AAPM 2026.
- **Cancer screening decisions:** A decision-analytic model for optimizing the diagnostic management of screen-detected pulmonary nodules; accepted for presentation at WCLC 2026.
- **Approximate dynamic programming:** State-relevance weights, shape-constrained approximate linear programming, and decomposition algorithms for large Markov decision processes.
